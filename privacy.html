隱私權政策

Fairy Genius（Fairy Prompt）｜最後更新：2026 年 10 月 23 日

適用範圍

本政策適用於 Fairy Genius 提供的服務：Fairy Prompt（fairyprompt.com），以及 fairygenius.com 上的作品。

用 Google 帳號登入時，我們取得的資料

你選擇「用 Google 登入」時，我們只向 Google 取得登入所需的基本資料：電子郵件地址、名稱和大頭貼網址。我們用電子郵件確認你是已登記的使用者，然後讓你進入自己的帳號。我們不會讀取你的 Gmail、雲端硬碟、聯絡人或其他 Google 資料，也不會代表你在 Google 上做任何事。

你在服務裡建立的資料

你在服務裡輸入或上傳的內容（例如 Prompt、照片），只用來提供服務給你本人，只有你的帳號看得到。學生資料不會拿去訓練 AI，不會做統計分析，也不會提供給第三方。

資料存放與保護

資料存放在我們使用的雲端資料庫服務（Supabase）。資料庫只能經過驗證登入身分的程式讀寫，傳輸全程加密。上傳給 AI 讀取的照片（例如做頭像用的照片）只在處理期間暫存，處理完就刪除。

分享與出售

我們不出售你的個人資料，也不會分享給廣告商。只有在法律要求時才會提供資料給主管機關。

Cookie 與追蹤

我們不使用廣告追蹤或第三方分析 Cookie。網站只在你的瀏覽器保存登入狀態和你的偏好設定。

刪除資料

你可以隨時要求刪除你的帳號和所有資料，請寫信到下面的電子郵件，我們會在 30 天內處理。

Privacy Policy

Fairy Genius (Fairy Prompt) · Last updated: 23 October 2026

Scope

This policy covers the services provided by Fairy Genius: Fairy Prompt (fairyprompt.com) and the works published on fairygenius.com.

Information we receive when you sign in with Google

When you choose “Sign in with Google”, we receive only the basic profile information needed to sign you in: your email address, name and profile picture URL. We use your email address to confirm that you are a registered user and to open your own account. We do not access your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts or any other Google data, and we never act on your behalf in Google services.

Information you create in the services

Content you enter or upload (such as prompts, photos) is used only to provide the service to you and is visible only to your account. Student information is never used to train AI models, never used for statistics, and never shared with third parties.

Storage and security

Data is stored with our cloud database provider (Supabase). The database can only be accessed through code that verifies your signed-in identity, and all traffic is encrypted. Photos sent for AI reading (such as photos for making an avatar) are stored only while they are being processed and are then deleted.

Sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal information or share it with advertisers. We disclose information to authorities only when required by law.

Cookies and tracking

We do not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics cookies. The sites store only your sign-in state and preferences in your browser.

Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your account and all of your data at any time by emailing the address below. We will act on your request within 30 days.
