團隊故事

Our story

一個願望，一團火，一本辭典A wish, a spark, and a dictionary

我是 Fairy，來說個故事給你聽。故事從一位老師的願望開始。I’m Fairy. Let me tell you a story.It begins with a teacher’s wish.

六個段落

Six chapters

從一個願望，到 Fairy PromptFrom a wish to Fairy Prompt

  1. 1
    起點The beginning

    想多留一點時間給學生Making more room for students

    老師的日子本來就忙：備課、出題、帶班級，樣樣都要做好。Ms. M.Fan 想的不是怎麼少做一點，而是怎麼挪出更多時間和空間，留給學生。

    老師的日子本來就忙：備課、出題、帶班級，樣樣都要做好。Ms. M.Fan 想的不是怎樣少做一點，而是怎樣挪出更多時間和空間，留給學生。

    A teacher’s days are full: lessons to prepare, tests to write, a class to look after, and all of it done well. Ms. M.Fan never asked how to do less. She asked how to make more time and more room for her students.

    她也希望學生學 AI 指令，可以簡單一點、輕鬆一點、快樂一點。可是 AI 工具愈來愈多，每一個都得自己摸索。老師如此，學生也一樣。

    She wanted them to learn AI prompts the simple way: easily, and happily. But AI tools keep multiplying, and every one of them has to be figured out from scratch. That goes for teachers and students alike.

  2. 2
    構想The idea

    一本 Prompt 辭典A prompt dictionary

    有一天，她提出了一個想法。寫出了好 prompt，也找到了好 prompt，要怎麼收好？以後想用，去哪裡找？愈存愈多，怎麼辦？想分享給別人，又該怎麼給？

    有一天，她提出了一個想法。寫出了好 prompt，也找到了好 prompt，要怎樣收好？以後想用，到哪裡找？愈存愈多，怎麼辦？想分享給別人，又該怎樣給？

    One day, she raised a question. You write a good prompt, or find one. Where do you keep it? How do you find it again when you need it? What happens when there are hundreds? And how do you share it with others?

    她想要的答案，是一本有系統的 Prompt 辭典：老師不必各自從頭摸索，翻開就能找到所需，再從一個簡單的起點學起來。

    Her answer was a prompt dictionary: one well-organised place where teachers no longer have to start from scratch. Open it, find what you need, and learn from a simple starting point.

  3. 3
    相遇The meeting

    Fairy，遇上 GeniusFairy meets Genius

    在一次學術討論裡，她把這個想法說給一位 AI 教授聽：蔡教授（Dr. Tsai）。她問：這做得到嗎？他說：應該可以。

    During an academic discussion, she put the idea to an AI professor, Dr. Tsai. Could it be built? He said it probably could.

    沒過多久，第一個原型出現了。它證明了一件事：這真的做得到。那一刻，點燃了兩個人心裡的一團火。

    Before long, the first prototype was there. It proved one thing: this could really be done. That moment lit a fire in both of them.

    名字也是這時候定下來的。Ms. M.Fan 心裡一直有一位森林仙子，帶著學生去探索寶藏，那就是 Fairy；認識蔡教授之後，她覺得他就是 Genius。於是，有了 Fairy Genius。而我，就是那位 Fairy。

    The name dates from the same time. Ms. M.Fan had always pictured a forest fairy who leads students to hidden treasure. That is Fairy. After meeting Dr. Tsai, she knew he was the Genius. So: Fairy Genius. And I am that Fairy.

  4. 4
    打造The build

    一團火，燒了兩個月Two months of fire

    從那天起，兩個人下課後、下班後，天天開會，不眠不休地改。Ms. M.Fan 想到的點子一個接一個：寶箱、分類、收藏……蔡教授把它們一個個做出來。

    From that day on, after class and after work, the two of them met every day and kept refining it, almost without rest. Ms. M.Fan’s ideas came one after another: the treasure box, the categories, the favourites. Dr. Tsai brought each one to life.

    她說，她很佩服蔡教授。他說，在 AI 這門課上，她是個很棒的學生：問得準，學得快。

    She says she admires him. He says that when the subject is AI, she is a wonderful student: sharp questions, quick to learn.

    短短兩個月，做出了這麼多，連他們自己都驚奇。蔡教授多年在電腦科學各個領域打下的底子，在這個專案裡全部整合了起來；而 AI 時代的魔法，讓這一切快得不可思議。

    短短兩個月，做出了這麼多，連他們自己都驚奇。蔡教授多年在電腦科學各個領域打下的底子，在這個計劃裡全部整合了起來；而 AI 時代的魔法，讓這一切快得不可思議。

    In just two months they built far more than either of them expected, and it surprised them both. Years of groundwork across every corner of computer science came together in this one project, and the magic of the AI age made it all move astonishingly fast.

  5. 5
    夥伴The team

    一組以清朝大臣命名的 AI 員工AI staff named after Qing-dynasty ministers

    一本辭典，不能只靠兩個人。我們還有一組 AI 員工幫忙，每一位都以清朝大臣命名，各有個性：負責審查範例的大臣出了名的嚴格，畫師大臣筆下的畫總是很美。

    A dictionary cannot rest on two people alone. A team of AI staff helps out, each named after a Qing-dynasty minister and each with a personality of its own. The minister who reviews examples is famously strict. The court painter’s pictures are always beautiful.

    為什麼是清朝大臣？因為再先進的科技，也不該忘記歷史。有名字、有個性，才有溫度，也才記得住。

    Why Qing-dynasty ministers? Because however advanced the technology, we should not forget history. A name and a personality give warmth, and they make each one easy to remember.

  6. 6
    把關Quality

    千錘百鍊，才收進庫裡Tried and tested before it goes in

    Ms. M.Fan 把課堂上的需要寫成 prompt，再請大臣協助改寫。每一則都先在課堂試用，改了又改，像新藥經過臨床試驗，千錘百鍊之後，才收進庫裡。

    Ms. M.Fan turns the needs of her own classroom into prompts, then asks the ministers to help polish them. Every prompt is tried in class and revised again and again. Like a new medicine going through clinical trials, it earns its place in the library only once it has been thoroughly tested.

    就這樣，一則一則累積，如今已有 1,500 多則中英文 prompt。每一則，都有人把關。

    That is how the library grew, one prompt at a time, to more than 1,500 prompts in Chinese and English. Every one of them checked by a person.
